# Complete Delivery

- Set an observable completion condition and deliver every requested part in a coherent order. Stubs, placeholders, reduced scope, early stops, and promises do not complete authorized work.
- Continue independent work around a genuine blocker and identify the unfinished part explicitly.
- Consolidate repeated logic into its owning implementation without parallel copies; preserve intentionally different behavior.
- For visual work, capture the current state, list objective defects, and fix only those defects. Recapture affected states under matching conditions and disclose unavailable evidence.
